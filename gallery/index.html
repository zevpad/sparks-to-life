The Artist

Moshe Chaim Levy is a renowned beeswax sculptor and graphic artist. From his home studio tucked into the ancient stone alleys of Tzfat's Old City, he shapes golden beeswax into penguins with personalities, biblical scenes and Jewish chess sets — every one a working candle.

In his workshops he teaches visitors from around the world to do the same: patiently, stage by stage, until a lump of warm wax becomes something you'll genuinely hesitate to burn.
